Reaction Quotient and Equilibrium Constant

The reaction quotient Q has the same form as the equilibrium constant K, but you can calculate it at any moment. K is the value Q has once the system reaches equilibrium.

Q1How do I write the expression?Products over reactants, each raised to its coefficient
Q2What gets left out?Pure solids and pure liquids
Q3What’s the difference between Q and K?Q at any moment, K only at equilibrium
