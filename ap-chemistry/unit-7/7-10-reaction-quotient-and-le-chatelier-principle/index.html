Reaction Quotient and Le Châtelier’s Principle

Le Châtelier’s principle is a prediction; Q and K are the explanation. Most stresses change Q, and the system shifts until Q equals K again. Temperature is different because it changes K itself.

Q1Why does the system shift?A stress makes Q no longer equal to K
Q2Which way does it go?Q < K shifts toward products; Q > K shifts toward reactants
Q3Why is temperature different?Temperature changes the value of K
