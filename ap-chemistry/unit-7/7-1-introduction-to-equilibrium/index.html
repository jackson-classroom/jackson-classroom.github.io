Introduction to Equilibrium

At equilibrium, a reaction hasn’t stopped. The forward and reverse reactions are running at the same rate, so the concentrations stop changing.

Q1Has the reaction stopped?Dynamic equilibrium
Q2What’s equal at equilibrium?The forward and reverse rates, not the concentrations
Q3How can I tell it’s there?Concentration and rate graphs that level off
