Introduction to Le Châtelier’s Principle

Disturb a system at equilibrium and it shifts to partly undo the change. Le Châtelier’s principle predicts which way it goes when you change concentration, pressure, volume, or temperature.

Q1What counts as a stress?Changes in concentration, pressure, volume, or temperature
Q2Which way will it shift?The direction that partly offsets the change
Q3What doesn’t shift it?Adding a catalyst, or an inert gas at constant volume
