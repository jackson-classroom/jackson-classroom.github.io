Properties of the Equilibrium Constant

Change how a reaction is written and K changes in a predictable way. These rules let you find K for a new reaction by combining reactions you already know.

Q1What if I reverse the reaction?The new K is 1/K
Q2What if I multiply it?Raise K to that power
