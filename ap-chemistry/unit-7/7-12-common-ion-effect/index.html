Common-Ion Effect

Dissolve a salt in a solution that already contains one of its ions and less of it dissolves. Le Châtelier’s principle explains why, and Ksp tells you how much less.

Q1Why does less dissolve?The shared ion pushes the equilibrium toward the solid
Q2Does Ksp change?No. The solubility changes; Ksp doesn’t
Q3How much less dissolves?Molar solubility with a common ion present
