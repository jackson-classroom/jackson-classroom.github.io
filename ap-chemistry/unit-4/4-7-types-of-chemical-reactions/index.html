Types of Chemical Reactions

Most reactions in AP Chemistry fall into three families: acid–base, oxidation–reduction, and precipitation. Recognizing the family tells you what’s actually being transferred or formed.

Q1Is a proton changing hands?Acid–base reactions
Q2Are electrons changing hands?Oxidation–reduction (redox) reactions
Q3Is a solid forming from solution?Precipitation reactions
