Introduction for Reactions

Bubbles, a new solid, a color change, a jump in temperature: each is a clue that new substances formed. This topic is about telling a chemical change from a physical one using what you can observe.

Q1Did a new substance form?The difference between a physical change and a chemical change
Q2What counts as evidence?Gas formation, a precipitate, a color change, heat or light
Q3Can the evidence fool you?Physical changes that look like reactions, such as the bubbles in boiling water
