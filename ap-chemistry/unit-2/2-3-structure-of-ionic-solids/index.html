Structure of Ionic Solids

An ionic solid isn’t a collection of separate molecules. It’s a repeating lattice of cations and anions, each ion held in place by the attraction of its neighbors.

Q1How are the ions arranged?Alternating cations and anions in a three-dimensional lattice
Q2What holds it together?Coulombic attraction between oppositely charged neighbors
Q3How do I draw it?Particle diagrams that show relative ion sizes and charges
