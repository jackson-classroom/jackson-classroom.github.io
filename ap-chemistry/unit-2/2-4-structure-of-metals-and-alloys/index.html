Structure of Metals and Alloys

A metal is a lattice of cations surrounded by a sea of shared valence electrons. That model explains why metals conduct and bend, and why mixing in a second element changes their properties.

Q1Why do metals conduct and bend?Delocalized electrons in the electron sea model
Q2What if the atoms are similar in size?Substitutional alloys, like brass
Q3What if one atom is much smaller?Interstitial alloys, like steel
