Properties of Solids

Diamond, table salt, ice, and copper are all solids, but they behave very differently. What the particles are and what holds them together predicts hardness, melting point, and conductivity.

Q1What kind of solid is it?Ionic, molecular, metallic, and covalent network solids
Q2Why does diamond outlast ice?Melting point and hardness from the forces holding the solid together
Q3Does it conduct?Mobile charges in metals, molten salts, and dissolved ions
