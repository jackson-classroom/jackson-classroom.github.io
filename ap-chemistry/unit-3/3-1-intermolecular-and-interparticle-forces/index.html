Intermolecular and Interparticle Forces

Intermolecular forces are the attractions between particles, not the bonds inside them. Their type and strength explain boiling points, melting points, and why some substances mix while others don’t.

Q1What attracts one molecule to another?Dispersion, dipole–dipole, hydrogen bonding, and ion–dipole forces
Q2Why are dispersion forces sometimes strongest?Polarizability and the number of electrons
Q3Which one boils higher?Ranking substances by the strength of their attractions
