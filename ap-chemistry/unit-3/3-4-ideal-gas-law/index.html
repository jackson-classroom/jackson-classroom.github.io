Ideal Gas Law

Pressure, volume, temperature, and amount of a gas are tied together by one equation. Use it to find any one of them from the others, and handle mixtures of gases through partial pressures.

Q1How do P, V, n, and T relate?The ideal gas law, PV = nRT
Q2What about a mixture of gases?Dalton’s law of partial pressures
Q3What share of the pressure is mine?Mole fraction and each gas’s partial pressure
