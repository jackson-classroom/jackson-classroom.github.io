Energy of Phase Changes

Melting and boiling take energy even though the temperature doesn’t change. That energy goes into pulling particles away from each other against their intermolecular attractions.

Q1Where does the energy go?Overcoming intermolecular attractions
Q2Why doesn’t the temperature change?Constant temperature during melting and boiling
