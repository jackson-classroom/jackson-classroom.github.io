Bond Enthalpies

Breaking a bond always costs energy and forming a bond always releases it. Compare the energy spent breaking bonds with the energy released forming new ones and you can estimate ΔH.

Q1Does breaking a bond release energy?No. Breaking bonds costs energy; forming bonds releases it
Q2Will the reaction release energy overall?Comparing bonds broken with bonds formed
Q3What’s ΔH?ΔH ≈ Σ(bonds broken) − Σ(bonds formed)
