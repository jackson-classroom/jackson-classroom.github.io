Heat Transfer and Thermal Equilibrium

Put a hot object in contact with a cold one and energy flows from hot to cold until their temperatures match. At the particle level, that flow is the result of countless collisions.

Q1Which way does heat flow?From higher temperature to lower temperature
Q2How does it happen at the particle level?Energy transfer through particle collisions
Q3When does it stop?Thermal equilibrium, when the temperatures are equal
