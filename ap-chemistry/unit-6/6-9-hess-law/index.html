Hess’s Law

Enthalpy is a state function, so the path doesn’t matter. Hess’s law lets you build a reaction you can’t measure out of reactions you can, adding up their ΔH values as you go.

Q1Why doesn’t the path matter?Enthalpy as a state function
Q2What happens to ΔH when I flip or multiply?Reversing changes the sign; multiplying scales ΔH
Q3How do I build the target reaction?Combining reactions so the extra species cancel
