Heat Capacity and Calorimetry

Some substances heat up quickly and others barely change temperature. Specific heat capacity measures that difference, and calorimetry uses it to measure the heat a process releases or absorbs.

Q1Why does water heat up so slowly?Specific heat capacity
Q2How much heat was that?q = mcΔT
Q3How do I measure it in the lab?Calorimetry: heat lost by one part equals heat gained by the other
