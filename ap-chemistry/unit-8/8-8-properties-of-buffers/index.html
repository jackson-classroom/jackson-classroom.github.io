Properties of Buffers

A buffer contains a weak acid to neutralize added base and a conjugate base to neutralize added acid. That’s why its pH barely moves when you add a little of either.

Q1Why doesn’t the pH change much?Each component neutralizes one kind of addition
Q2What reacts with added acid?The conjugate base
Q3What reacts with added base?The weak acid
