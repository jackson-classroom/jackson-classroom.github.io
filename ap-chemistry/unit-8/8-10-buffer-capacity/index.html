Buffer Capacity

Every buffer has a limit. Buffer capacity is how much acid or base a buffer can absorb before its pH starts to change sharply, and it depends on how concentrated the buffer’s components are.

Q1How much can it take?The amount of acid or base before the pH changes sharply
Q2Which buffer holds up better?Higher concentrations mean greater capacity
Q3Does the ratio matter?Capacity against added acid vs. added base
