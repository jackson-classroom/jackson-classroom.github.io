Henderson-Hasselbalch Equation

The Henderson-Hasselbalch equation gives a buffer’s pH from the pKa and the ratio of conjugate base to acid. It’s the fastest way to find a buffer’s pH or to design one for a target pH.

Q1What’s the buffer’s pH?pH = pKa + log([A−]/[HA])
Q2What happens after I add acid or base?Recalculating the ratio after the reaction
Q3How do I make a buffer at pH 5?Choosing an acid with a pKa near the target
