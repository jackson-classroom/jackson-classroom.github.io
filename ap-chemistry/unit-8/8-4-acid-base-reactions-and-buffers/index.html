Acid-Base Reactions and Buffers

When an acid and a base react, the pH depends on what’s left over. A weak acid mixed with its conjugate base, in comparable amounts, makes a buffer that resists changes in pH.

Q1What’s left after they react?Stoichiometry first, then equilibrium
Q2What’s the pH of the mixture?Mixtures of strong and weak acids and bases
Q3When does a mixture become a buffer?A weak acid and its conjugate base together
