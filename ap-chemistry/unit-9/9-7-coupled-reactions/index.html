Coupled Reactions

A reaction that isn’t thermodynamically favored can still be made to happen. Couple it to a strongly favored reaction, or supply energy from outside, and the overall process can go.

Q1How can an unfavored reaction happen?Coupling it to a favored one
Q2Is the combination favored?Adding the ΔG° values of the coupled reactions
Q3Where else can the energy come from?Light, electricity, and ATP in living systems
