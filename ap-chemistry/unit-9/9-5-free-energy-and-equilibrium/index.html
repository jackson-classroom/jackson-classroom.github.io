9.5 Free Energy and Equilibrium ΔG° = −RT ln K · the sign of ΔG° and the size of K · converting between ΔG° and K

Free Energy and Equilibrium

ΔG° and K describe the same thing in two ways. A negative ΔG° means K is greater than 1, and ΔG° = −RT ln K lets you convert one into the other.

Q1How are ΔG° and K connected?ΔG° = −RT ln K
Q2What does the sign tell me?A negative ΔG° means K > 1
Q3Why is K so huge or so tiny?Small changes in ΔG° make large changes in K
