Periodic Trends

Atomic radius, ionization energy, and electronegativity all follow patterns across the periodic table. Every one of those patterns comes back to how strongly the nucleus pulls on the outermost electrons.

Q1Why do the trends exist?Coulomb’s law: nuclear charge, shielding, and distance from the nucleus
Q2Which atom is bigger?Atomic and ionic radius across a period and down a group
Q3How hard is it to take an electron?Ionization energy, electron affinity, and electronegativity
