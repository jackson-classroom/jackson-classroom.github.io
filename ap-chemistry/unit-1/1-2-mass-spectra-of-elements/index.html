Mass Spectra of Elements

Most elements exist as a mix of isotopes. A mass spectrometer sorts atoms by mass and reports how common each one is, and that is where the average atomic mass on the periodic table comes from.

Q1What is a mass spectrum showing?Each peak is an isotope: its position gives the mass, its height gives the relative abundance
Q2Why isn’t atomic mass a whole number?Average atomic mass as a weighted average of the isotopes
Q3Which isotope is most common?Reasoning from a spectrum or an average mass back to the isotopes
