Collision Model

Particles have to collide to react, but most collisions do nothing. A collision only leads to a reaction if it has enough energy and the right orientation.

Q1Why don’t all collisions work?Enough energy and the right orientation
Q2Why does heating speed things up?More collisions, and more of them with enough energy
Q3What fraction of collisions can react?A Maxwell–Boltzmann distribution with the activation energy marked
