Elementary Reactions

An elementary reaction happens in a single collision or event. That’s why, for elementary steps only, you can write the rate law straight from the coefficients.

Q1What happens in one step?Elementary reactions as single molecular events
Q2How many particles are involved?Unimolecular, bimolecular, and termolecular steps
Q3Can I read the rate law from the equation?Yes, but only for an elementary step
