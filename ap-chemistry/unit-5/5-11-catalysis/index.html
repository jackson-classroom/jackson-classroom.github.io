Catalysis

A catalyst speeds up a reaction without being used up. It works by opening a new pathway with a lower activation energy, so it shows up in the mechanism but not in the overall equation.

Q1How does a catalyst speed things up?A new mechanism with a lower activation energy
Q2How do I spot one in a mechanism?Used up in one step and regenerated in a later one
Q3What kinds are there?Acid–base, surface, and enzyme catalysts
