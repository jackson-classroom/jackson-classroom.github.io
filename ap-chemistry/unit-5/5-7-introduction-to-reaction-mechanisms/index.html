Introduction to Reaction Mechanisms

Most reactions don’t happen in a single step. A mechanism is a proposed sequence of elementary steps, and the steps have to add up to the overall balanced equation.

Q1What really happens, step by step?Mechanisms as a series of elementary reactions
Q2What’s an intermediate?A species made in one step and used up in a later one
Q3Does the mechanism add up?Summing the steps to get the overall equation
