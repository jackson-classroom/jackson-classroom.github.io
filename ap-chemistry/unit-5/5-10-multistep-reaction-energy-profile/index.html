Multistep Reaction Energy Profile

A multistep reaction has a hill for every elementary step. The valleys between the hills are intermediates, and the step with the largest activation energy is the slow step.

Q1How many steps are there?Counting the hills and the intermediates between them
Q2Which step is slowest?The step with the largest activation energy
